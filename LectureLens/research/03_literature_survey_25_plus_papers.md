## 2. Category-by-Category Synthesis & Deep Comparative Analysis

### Category 1: Multimodal Learning & Educational Lecture Understanding
- **Prior Research State:** Seminal works including the **LPM Dataset (Lee et al., ICCV 2023)**, **SlideVQA (Tanaka et al., AAAI 2023)**, and **Lecture2Note (Xu et al., IEEE ICME 2019)** laid the empirical foundation for multimodal educational media. They demonstrated that educational video understanding requires jointly parsing visual slide layouts, embedded diagrams, and spoken audio.
- **Persistent Limitations:** Existing models (e.g. Video-ChatGPT, LPM baseline) rely on uniform vision-language token embeddings that treat all academic disciplines identically. In a calculus lecture, a mathematical symbol (e.g. $\partial f / \partial x$) carries vastly higher informational entropy than the instructor's background webcam feed. Furthermore, existing systems assume static, printed slides and fail on dynamic, handwritten blackboards.
- **LectureLens Solution:** 
  1. Implements Gemini Vision OCR with explicit blackboard cleanup prompts that extract clean **KaTeX / LaTeX** formulas and diagram structures.
  2. Synthesizes synchronized `MultimodalEvent` entities that unify visual snapshots with concurrent spoken utterances.
  3. Introduces 4 distinct study note modes (*Short, Detailed, Exam, Beginner*) rather than a single monolithic summary.

### Category 2: Speech Recognition & Speaker Attribution
- **Prior Research State:** **Whisper (Radford et al., ICML 2023)** established near-human speech transcription accuracy across diverse accents. **pyannote.audio 2.1 (Bredin, Interspeech 2023)** and **WhisperX (Bain et al., Interspeech 2023)** demonstrated that phoneme alignment and neural diarization achieve precise boundary timestamps.
- **Persistent Limitations:** Vanilla Whisper suffers from severe timestamp drift when transcribing long-form 60-minute audio streams, and lacks real-time speaker attribution. Neural diarization models (pyannote.audio) require heavy GPU compute that cannot run locally within low-latency interactive lecture rooms.
- **LectureLens Solution:**
  1. Employs a client-side audio slicing pipeline (3000ms Opus chunks) routed over WebSockets to port 5000 (`unifiedSttServer.js`).
  2. Bypasses expensive acoustic diarization by binding authenticated user identity from the client's WebRTC session directly to speech chunks, producing deterministic speaker tags (*Teacher* vs. *Student*) at zero extra compute cost.
  3. Provides live in-memory translation into 5 languages (Spanish, Hindi, Marathi, French, German).

### Category 3: OCR, Mathematical Expression & Diagram Extraction
- **Prior Research State:** **Nougat (Blecher et al., ICLR 2024)** demonstrated that Transformer vision encoders can transcribe academic PDFs directly into LaTeX math. **CoMER (Zhao & Gao, ECCV 2022)** utilized coverage attention to parse handwritten formulas, and **ChartQA (Masry et al., ACL 2022)** established visual QA benchmarks for charts.
- **Persistent Limitations:** Nougat is restricted to clean, digital PDF documents; CoMER processes isolated formula crops without understanding broader slide or whiteboard context; ChartQA is confined to standard 2D business plots (bar/line/pie) and cannot simulate interactive Computer Engineering concepts.
- **LectureLens Solution:**
  1. Dual-modal vision pipeline extracting both printed presentation slides and unstructured whiteboard notes.
  2. Implements SHA-256 frame deduplication to avoid redundant LLM vision calls during static slide presentations.
  3. Integrates an interactive **3D Educational Visualization Lab** (Three.js) capable of rendering complex procedural concepts (Raft quorums, AVL rotations, MLP manifolds) that 2D static images cannot adequately convey.

### Category 4: Cross-Modal & Temporal Sequence Alignment
- **Prior Research State:** **MaViLS (Anderer et al., Interspeech 2024)** and **CLIP4Clip (Luo et al., Neurocomputing 2022)** tackled video-to-text retrieval. **Yang et al. (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2022)** evaluated dynamic time warping (DTW) for sequence alignment.
- **Persistent Limitations:** Existing DTW and sequence alignment techniques enforce strict monotonic temporal progression ($j_{curr} \ge j_{prev}$). In university lectures, instructors routinely flip back to previous slides ($\Delta j < 0$) to answer student questions or clarify prerequisites. Monotonic algorithms catastrophically fail during such lookbacks.
- **LectureLens Solution (SALKG Core):**
  1. Formulates non-monotonic dynamic programming alignment with an asymmetric lookback transition penalty: $\Phi(j_{prev}, j_{curr}) = \Phi_{base} + \lambda_{back} (j_{prev} - j_{curr})$.
  2. Introduces subject-adaptive feature weighting ($\mathbf{w}(\omega)$) that automatically boosts symbol overlap in Mathematics and identifier overlap in Computer Science.
  3. Implements Beam Search Pruning ($B=5$) to achieve sub-second execution ($\approx 570\text{ ms}$) on 60-minute lectures.

### Category 5: Automated Educational Knowledge Graphs
- **Prior Research State:** **LectureBank (Li et al., AAAI 2019)** and **Irene Li et al. (ACL 2021)** pioneered prerequisite chain learning from educational slide decks. **MOOCCube (Yu et al., ACL 2020)** created a massive cross-course concept repository.
- **Persistent Limitations:** Prior models extract concept pairs exclusively from static slide text, ignoring spoken lectures. Furthermore, purely statistical or autoencoder-based prerequisite predictors frequently generate circular dependency cycles (e.g. *Concept A $\to$ Concept B $\to$ Concept A*), which violates pedagogical logic.
- **LectureLens Solution:**
  1. Binds every knowledge graph triple to an immutable multimodal provenance record ($P(e) = \{s_{id}, v_{id}, [t_0, t_1]\}$).
  2. Executes an automated DAG verification pass via **Kahn's Algorithm (Topological Sort)**, identifying and breaking circular prerequisite cycles to guarantee a cycle-free pedagogical DAG.

### Category 6: Grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- **Prior Research State:** **RAG (Lewis et al., NeurIPS 2020)** pioneered non-parametric dense retrieval for knowledge-intensive NLP. **ALCE (Gao et al., EMNLP 2023)** established citation benchmarks, and **Self-RAG (Asai et al., ICLR 2024)** introduced reflective retrieval tokens.
- **Persistent Limitations:** Standard RAG systems retrieve text from open web corpora or static documents without temporal grounding. In university education, a student asking *"What did the professor say about test cases?"* requires answers grounded strictly in *this specific lecture*, with exact video timestamps rather than generic textbook answers.
- **LectureLens Solution:**
  1. "Ask This Lecture" RAG binds queries exclusively to the indexed `transcript_segments` of the active lecture.
  2. Every generated claim includes an exact timestamp citation: `[Source: Lecture timestamp 14:32]`.
  3. Strict system boundaries command the model to explicitly state when a topic was unmentioned rather than speculating.

### Category 7: Hallucination Detection & Verification
- **Prior Research State:** **FActScore (Min et al., EMNLP 2023)** introduced atomic claim decomposition for biographical generation. **SelfCheckGPT (Manakul et al., EMNLP 2023)** detected hallucinations via sampling inconsistency, and **R-Tuning (Zhang et al., NAACL 2024)** taught LLMs to refuse unknown questions.
- **Persistent Limitations:** Existing hallucination detectors collapse all factual errors into a single scalar confidence score. In university lectures, this fails to distinguish between an assertion that was **faithfully taught by the lecturer but mathematically wrong** (a lecturer slip) versus an assertion that is **scientifically sound but completely out of syllabus** (unfaithful to the lecture).
- **LectureLens Solution (SALKG-Guard):**
  1. Implements the **Dual-Axis Independence Principle**, evaluating **Lecture Faithfulness** ($\mathcal{F}$) and **Domain Correctness** ($\mathcal{D}$) on orthogonal axes.
  2. Embeds deterministic subject validators (calculus derivative checkers, algorithm asymptotic complexity verifiers, circuit equation parsers).
  3. Provides a risk-calibrated decision engine with four distinct outcomes (`ACCEPT`, `REGENERATE`, `ABSTAIN`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`) and a bounded correction loop.

### Category 8: Real-Time Media SFU & Low-Latency Architecture
- **Prior Research State:** **Jansen et al. (ACM SIGMETRICS 2018)** and **Kim et al. (IEEE Access 2023)** proved that Selective Forwarding Units (SFUs) scale significantly better than peer-to-peer mesh WebRTC for multi-party video conferencing.
- **Persistent Limitations:** Existing WebRTC SFU evaluations focus solely on generic video/audio streams and do not address the complex coordination of dedicated high-resolution screen sharing, live canvas whiteboard capture, and concurrent speech-to-text chunking.
- **LectureLens Solution:**
  1. Implements **mediasoup v3** router managing dedicated screen share tracks delivered to an expansive primary stage on student clients.
  2. Decouples low-latency RTP packet routing (Node.js) from stateful persistence and AI orchestration (Spring Boot 3.3.4), ensuring that heavy AI summarization never causes video stream stutter.

### Category 9: Intelligent Learning Assessment & Spaced Repetition
- **Prior Research State:** **QG-Bench (Ushio et al., EMNLP 2022)** evaluated generative language models for automated paragraph question generation. **Settles & Meeder (ACL 2016)** formulated Half-Life Regression for spaced repetition memory decay.
- **Persistent Limitations:** Automated quiz tools routinely leak answer keys to the client browser in hidden fields, enabling trivial cheating. Furthermore, Half-Life Regression requires millions of student trials to fit its parameters, making it impractical for newly delivered lectures.
- **LectureLens Solution:**
  1. Anti-cheat quiz pipeline where answer keys are stripped from client payloads, and all grading is computed server-side in Spring Boot.
  2. Generates automated knowledge gap diagnostics highlighting specific weak conceptual areas.
  3. Implements an instant cold-start 5-box **Leitner System** for spaced-repetition flashcards.
